Your 3D prints, at a glance.

Printers it works with

The app talks straight to each printer on your network, using the interface the printer already has. No cloud login.

  • Bambu LabLocal MQTTIncluding the H2D. Uses the printer’s address, serial number and LAN access code.
  • Klipper and MoonrakerHTTPVoron and other Klipper printers running Moonraker. Fluidd and Mainsail addresses work too.
  • Creality K1 familyWebSocketK1, K1 Max and K1C with the stock status service. No Moonraker install needed.
  • OctoPrintHTTPEnder and other Marlin printers, through an OctoPrint host and an API key.
  • PrusaLinkHTTPPrusa printers with PrusaLink, using the local API key or username and password.
  • Duet and RepRapFirmwareHTTPDuet Web Control and Duet Software Framework. Monitoring only.
  • Elegoo (SDCP v3)WebSocketNetworked resin printers that use SDCP v3, including some Saturn and Mars models.

Tested on real printers so far: a Bambu Lab H2D and a Creality K1 Max. The other connections pass automated tests and still need real printers, so if you try one, please report from the app whether it works, partly works or doesn’t. Results depend on firmware.

What it does

01

Widgets with a camera snapshot

Put your printers on the Home Screen, the Lock Screen or the Mac desktop. Widgets show progress, time left and temperatures, and larger ones add a recent camera snapshot with how old it is. Home Screen and desktop widgets can pause or resume a print.

02

A print in your Mac menu bar

Show a ring, a percentage or the time left in the menu bar. Click it for each printer’s progress, temperatures and camera, then pause, resume or stop a job. Stop always asks first. Close the app’s window and the menu bar keeps watching.

Good to know

  • It connects only to the printers you add, on your own network. There’s no account, no cloud service and no analytics, and access codes stay in your device’s Keychain.
  • Away from home it needs a way to reach the printer, such as a VPN into your home network. A printer maker’s cloud account isn’t enough.
  • There are no push notifications. The open app checks about every 15 seconds. Apple decides when widgets refresh and may delay them, so every reading shows when it arrived.
  • Camera snapshots need a camera the app can reach on your network. Some Bambu Lab cameras need a separate camera relay, and A1 and P1 cameras aren’t supported.
  • Pause, resume and stop work on Bambu Lab, Klipper, OctoPrint, PrusaLink, Creality K1 and SDCP printers, if the firmware allows it. Duet is monitoring only, and the app never starts a print.

Support

In the app, open Settings and help, then Report a problem. On a Mac you can also use Help, then Report a Problem. Say whether a printer works, partly works or doesn’t. A report can attach a diagnostics file that you preview first and can turn off. It never includes printer names, addresses, serial numbers, access codes, job names or camera images.

Read the support guide for common connection problems, or email support@danielyakobian.com.

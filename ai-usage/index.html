AI Usage support

Track what you used and how much of your plan is left, right on your Mac desktop.

Get help

Email support@danielyakobian.com. Include your macOS version, the tool you connected, and what the widget shows. Please leave out prompts, credentials and account details.

Set up your first widget

  1. Open AI Usage and choose the tools you use. To explore with sample numbers, choose Preview with sample data.
  2. Connect a history folder to count requests and tokens, or connect a plan to see the quota it reports. Either choice works on its own. A dashboard is optional.
  3. Control-click the desktop, choose Edit Widgets, and search for AI Usage. Add AI plan limits for quota or AI usage totals for recorded activity.

Where the numbers come from

Claude. Choose your Claude Code history folder for local activity. For remaining quota, choose Connect Claude Desktop and approve the suggested folder. The widget shows the last reading the desktop app saved, with its observation time. An interactive Claude Code terminal can also share the limits it reports.

ChatGPT and Codex. Choose the Codex history folder for local activity and any recent limit readings recorded in a session. You can also sign in with OpenAI from AI Usage to request your account limits.

Google and Gemini. Choose the Google Antigravity history folder for recorded model usage. For remaining quota, open and sign in to the Antigravity desktop app on this Mac, then choose Connect Google quota in AI Usage. Approve the folder if macOS asks. AI Usage reads the model groups and reset times Antigravity provides. Terminal can stay closed.

If a widget is waiting for data

Open AI Usage and check the connection for that tool. A new history folder may have no recorded activity yet. A plan may have no current quota reading. For Google quota, keep Antigravity running. The Widgets section in Settings shows the readings available to your widgets.

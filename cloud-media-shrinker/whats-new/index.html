What's new

What changed in each version, newest first, with when it came out and where. What's coming covers what's next.

Latest1.2.2#Copy a link to 1.2.2

  • Mac App StoreNovember 23, 2026
  • Free download for MacNovember 23, 2026
  • iPhone and iPadIn Apple's review

On iPhone and iPad, these changes arrive once Apple approves the update.

Fixes

  • Videos with spatial audio sound right. Copies of videos with more than one audio track, like spatial audio on newer iPhones, could sound hollow. A copy now plays the same audio as its original. After you update, compress those videos again from their originals.
  • Long queues no longer slow to a crawl or start failing partway through.
  • MacA stuck video no longer holds up the queue. If one stops making progress for 10 minutes, it starts over once by itself. If it gets stuck again, it's marked so you can try it later, and the rest carries on.
  • Copies of iPhone videos keep their lens details in Photos, such as 26 mm f/1.6. Slo-mo videos are the exception.
  • Very short Slo-mo videos show 0:01 instead of a length of 0:00.
  • Photos are no longer kept with "caption differs" when their caption or keywords had been removed in Photos.

New and changed

  • No need to empty Recently Deleted. The app now explains that Recently Deleted doesn't count toward your iCloud storage. Deleted items can still take space on your iPhone, iPad or Mac for up to 30 days.
  • Balanced is the recommended preset, 1080p at high quality, and where new users start. If you already use the app, your preset stays as it is.
  • Presets run from most to least savings: Max Savings, Balanced, Keep 4K.
  • Slo-mo videos are easier to spot. They're marked on the cards and have their own filter, and the app tells you what changes before you compress one: the copy plays the same, but Photos can't change the slow part afterwards.
  • iPhoneA simpler compress screen. The presets sit side by side, so Delete originals and the size estimate fit on the same screen.
  • MacThe Photos Library picker opens in your Pictures folder on macOS 26 and older, and says what to select.
  • MacA Help button next to Quick tour.
  • The Family option is easier to find on the unlock screen.
  • Rate the app from Settings on iPhone or the Help menu on Mac.

The free download for Mac has the Mac changes too, except three that only the App Store version has: the Photos Library picker, the Family option and Rate.

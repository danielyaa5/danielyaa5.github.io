How to measure your threshold

You need a ruler or tape measure with millimeter or 1/16 inch marks, a straightedge (a level or a stiff ruler works) and your phone camera. Millimeters are best. Write down what you actually measure and don't subtract any clearance yourself.

  1. Threshold height (H)Stand the ruler upright on the lower finished floor and measure straight up to the top of the threshold. Check the left, center and right, and send all three readings if they differ.
  2. Doorway width (W)Measure the clear opening between the inside faces of the door jambs, where the ramp will sit. Send the real opening without taking anything off.
  3. Edge shapeLook at the front edge of the threshold at floor level and note whether it's square, angled or rounded (see below).
  4. Floor space (D) and door clearance (G)Measure the open floor in front of the threshold, from its base into the room. The ramp reaches about 3.4 times the threshold height into the room, so a 1 in threshold needs roughly 3.4 in of open floor. Note which way the door swings and the smallest gap under the door or door sweep along that path.
  5. Robot and sidesWrite down your robot vacuum's brand and model. If you need a ramp on both sides of the doorway, measure each side on its own and label them Side A and Side B.

Square, angled or rounded edge

The front edge decides how the ramp meets the threshold, so check it closely. Then send a close side-on photo at floor level with a ruler next to it, plus a photo of the whole doorway from above.

Good to know before you order

  • It's made for indoor robot vacuum use. It isn't a mobility ramp and isn't load rated.
  • Robots climb differently. Climbing ability, traction and clearance vary by model and threshold, which is why your robot's model is part of the order.
  • A custom ramp doesn't automatically mean every door will clear it, so check the gap under the door before ordering.
  • Keep PLA away from excessive heat and hot direct sunlight.

Questions

Do I need a ramp on both sides of the doorway?

One ramp set covers one side of a doorway. If you need both sides, measure each side separately, label them Side A and Side B, and send both so both size options can be confirmed.

My threshold is exactly 0.9 in. Which option do I pick?

Over 0.6 to 0.9 in. Each height range above 0.6 in leaves out its lower number and includes its upper one, so a measurement right on the line goes in the lower range.

My threshold is taller than 1.6 in. Can I get a ramp?

Not with the current size options, which stop at 1.6 in (40.64 mm). Send me your measurements and photos anyway, and I'll let you know if I can make one for your threshold.

How far does the ramp reach into the room?

About 3.4 times the threshold height, because it's designed around a 16 degree climb. A 1 in threshold needs roughly 3.4 in (86 mm) of open floor in front of it, and a 1.6 in threshold roughly 5.5 in (140 mm). Final dimensions depend on your threshold and are confirmed from your measurements.

Will it work with my Roborock, Roomba, Eufy, Dreame or Ecovacs?

Send your robot vacuum's brand and model with your measurements so I can check the fit. Climbing ability, traction and clearance vary by model and threshold. I'm not affiliated with these brands.

Will my door still close over the ramp?

Not automatically. Measure the smallest gap under the door or door sweep along its swing path, and send a photo if the door passes over where the ramp will sit, so clearance can be checked before you order.

Should I measure in inches or millimeters?

Millimeters if you can, or inches to the nearest 1/16. Send the actual measurements without subtracting any clearance.

How does the ramp stay in place?

It comes with double-sided mounting tape.

How long does it take to ship?

Each ramp is made to order and ships within 3 business days of purchase. Standard shipping within the US is included.

Can I return it?

Because each ramp is made to your measurements, change-of-mind returns aren't accepted. If it doesn't match the measurements you sent, or it arrives damaged, I'll reprint it or refund you. This doesn't limit Etsy's buyer protections.

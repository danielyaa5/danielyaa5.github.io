QuickDesk Privacy

Last updated October 30, 2026

QuickDesk is a macOS file browser and screenshot utility by Daniel Yakobian. No QuickDesk account is required.

Files and screenshots

QuickDesk works with files in folders you choose. Folder permissions and preferences are stored on your Mac. Screenshots are saved to your chosen folder. If you enable copying, the captured image is also placed on the macOS clipboard. QuickDesk does not upload your files or screenshots to us unless you explicitly attach screenshots to a support report and send it. A folder or clipboard service you separately use, such as cloud storage or Universal Clipboard, may sync that content under its own settings.

Frequently used apps

QuickDesk learns which apps you use to populate its optional app shelf. This history stays on your Mac and is not included in support reports.

Permissions

QuickDesk uses macOS folder access to browse and manage the folders you select. Screen Recording permission is used for screenshot capture. You can disable QuickDesk screenshot shortcuts in Settings and manage Screen Recording permission in macOS System Settings.

Analytics and tracking

QuickDesk does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs. It does not automatically transmit usage data, crash logs, file names, or screen captures to us.

Optional support reports

The in-app bug and feedback form sends only when you choose Send report. It includes your written report, optional reply email, up to three screenshots you select, and app diagnostics you can review or exclude. Diagnostics contain app and macOS versions and recent fixed app events from the current session; they exclude file paths, clipboard contents, typed text and device identifiers. Selected screenshots are resized and stripped of source filenames and metadata, but their visible content remains. Review them before sending.

Reports pass through Cloudflare and Resend to support@danielyakobian.com. The relay does not store report bodies; the email service and support mailbox process and retain messages to investigate problems and respond. Reports are not posted publicly or used for advertising. Do not include passwords or other private information.

Your choices

You can change the screenshot folder, disable clipboard copying, and turn off screenshot shortcuts in QuickDesk Settings. Files you saved remain yours to manage. To ask about a support message you sent or request its deletion, email support@danielyakobian.com.

Purchases and trial

Apple processes the free trial and permanent unlock through in-app purchases. QuickDesk checks signed purchase records on your Mac to determine access and the original trial end date. We do not receive your payment details or send your purchase history to our own server. Restoring a trial does not restart it.

Apple services

App Store purchases, downloads, and any diagnostics you choose to share with Apple are handled under Apple's terms and privacy settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@danielyakobian.com.

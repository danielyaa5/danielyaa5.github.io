QuickDesk Support

A customizable menu-bar file shelf and screenshot utility for macOS 14 or later.

Trial and permanent unlock

Download QuickDesk free and choose Start 7-day free trial to try every feature. After seven days, a one-time in-app purchase unlocks it permanently ($4.99 in the U.S.; local prices vary). There is no subscription or automatic charge. Restore Purchases recovers an existing unlock or the time left on your original trial. When a trial ends, the QuickDesk file browser and screenshot capture require an unlock. Your files remain accessible in Finder.

Open your files

Launch QuickDesk and click its menu-bar icon, or press Command-Escape. Choose a folder in Settings to grant access. You can customize the grid, list, file-type columns, preview, and hot corner. Press Command-Shift-F inside the popup to open the current folder in Finder.

Set up screenshots

In Settings → Screenshots, enable “Let QuickDesk handle screenshot shortcuts” and choose a save folder. Grant QuickDesk Screen Recording permission in macOS System Settings if prompted. Command-Shift-2 captures the active window, Command-Shift-3 captures the display under the pointer, and Command-Shift-4 captures a selected area. “Copy screenshots to clipboard” controls whether captures are also copied.

A shortcut or folder is not working

QuickDesk must be running for its shortcuts to work. If another utility already uses the same keys, change that utility's shortcut or disable its binding. For a folder that is no longer accessible, choose it again through QuickDesk Settings. After changing Screen Recording permission, relaunch QuickDesk if macOS asks.

Report a bug

Use Settings → Support → Report a bug, or email support@danielyakobian.com. Describe what happened, how to reproduce it, and what you expected. Include your QuickDesk and macOS versions. Review the message before sending; no report is sent automatically. Remove private information from any screenshots or files you choose to attach.
